About me

I'm Fibi, a 30-year-old frontend developer. I like the part of the job where a design turns into something that works — on every screen, in every browser, for whoever has to edit it next.

Most of my work sits between two worlds: hand-built interfaces in HTML, Tailwind and React, and production WordPress sites built with Timber, ACF and Elementor. I've shipped new builds, taken over existing sites, and spent a lot of hours tracking down the bug nobody else could find.

Core frontend

Semantic HTML, modern CSS, Tailwind, and vanilla JavaScript. Responsive, accessible layouts that match the design file.
